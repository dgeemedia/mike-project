Ask Us Anything

Do you offer free quotes?

+

Yes, absolutely. We offer free, no-obligation quotes for all projects. Simply fill in our contact form or call us and we'll arrange a convenient time to visit your property, assess the work and provide a detailed, itemised quote.

What areas do you cover?

+

We are based in Crewe, Cheshire and serve the surrounding areas including Nantwich, Wistaston, Winsford, Sandbach, Congleton, and across Cheshire and the North West. Please get in touch if you're unsure whether we cover your area.

Are you fully licensed and insured?

+

Yes. Mikes Constructions Group Ltd is fully licensed, accredited by leading industry bodies, and carries full public liability insurance. You can have complete peace of mind that all work is carried out professionally and safely.

How long will my project take?

+

Timelines vary depending on the scope of work. A bathroom renovation typically takes 1–2 weeks, a kitchen remodel 2–3 weeks, and a full property renovation can take several months. We provide a clear timeline with every quote and keep you updated throughout the project.

Can you work around us if we're living in the property?

+

Absolutely. We are experienced in working around homeowners who are living on site. We respect your home, clean up each day, and minimise disruption as much as possible. We'll agree a working schedule with you that suits your routine.

Do you handle planning permission?

+

We can advise you on whether your project requires planning permission and assist with the process. For most bathroom and kitchen renovations, planning permission is not required. For extensions, loft conversions and structural changes, we will guide you through the requirements.

What payment terms do you offer?

+

We typically require a deposit to secure your start date, with staged payments throughout the project based on milestones. The full payment schedule is detailed in your written quote before any work begins. We never ask for full payment upfront.

Do you provide a guarantee on your work?

+

Yes. We stand behind the quality of our workmanship. We provide a written guarantee on our labour and work closely with you to ensure you're completely satisfied before the project is signed off. We also only use quality materials from trusted suppliers.

Can you supply materials or should I buy my own?

+

We can do both. We can source and supply all materials — often at trade prices — or we can work with materials you've already purchased. We'll discuss the best approach during the quoting stage based on your project and preferences.
