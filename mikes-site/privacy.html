Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

Mikes Constructions Group Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is a construction and refurbishment company based in Crewe, Cheshire. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data when you use our website or contact us.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you submit an enquiry or contact us:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Details about your project or property
  • Your location/address for site visits
  • Any other information you choose to provide

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Manage your project once work has been agreed
  • Send you relevant updates about your project
  • Improve our website and services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties. We may share data with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our business (e.g. email providers), subject to confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined above, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via our contact page.

7. Cookies

This website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at info@mikes-constructions.co.uk.
